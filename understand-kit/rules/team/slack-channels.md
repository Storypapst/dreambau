# Slack channels (sunflowercare.slack.com)

Two projects share this workspace. ORISO channels start with `oriso-`; WeCare Remote (WCR)
channels start with `wcr-`. Always pick the ORISO one for ORISO work. Dictated names are
fuzzy — "Orizo", "Urizo", "code review" as two words — normalise them to the table below.

**Never post automatically.** Draft the message, show the full text to the person running
the session, and let them send it. The draft is written for *them*, in their voice — look up
their Slack ID in `team-directory.md`; never sign or mention as someone else. English only.
