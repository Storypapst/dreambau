# Production `64k` — "Skyline" (budget: 65 536 bytes minified)

Read `common.md` and `../CONTRACT.md` first. File: `src/p/64k.js`. This is the showpiece: cinematic, fully lit, with
a full score. Custom GL (`init`/`draw`) with post-processing through `D.rt`, or a single `frag` if you prefer; set `px`
(about 1.2e6) because the picture is raymarched.

## Picture: a construction site at blue hour becomes a skyline at sunrise

A raymarched 3D city: ground plane plus a grid of buildings. For robustness and speed evaluate **one** building per cell
(`id = floor(p.xz / cell)`, box with hash-driven footprint and height, clip each step by the distance to the cell border so a
single box SDF per step is valid); a taller, stepped hero tower stands in the middle. Floors appear in whole storeys
(quantised height), growth waves run outwards from the centre, one ring per bar. While a building grows it carries a thin
scaffold cage (cheap bar SDF) and orange work lights; when it is finished the cage vanishes and a glass facade with lit
windows fades in. Lighting: sun/sky colour ramp over the whole piece, soft shadows, ambient occlusion (a few taps),
analytic height fog and haze, a few cheap god-ray steps toward the sun, bloom, filmic tone mapping, vignette, and film grain
whose strength falls from strong (noise) to nearly none (order): the theme in one parameter.

Palette: deep blue-teal blue hour → copper and orange sunrise → warm pale daylight on glass and steel. Cinematic, restrained.

Phases (guidance; put changes on bar lines):

1. **Night noise (0 to ~13 s).** Almost black, heavy grain, drifting dust motes and fog in the beam of a few work lights; the
   faint foundation grid blinks on the ground; low rumble and distant metal clangs. Camera low and slow.
2. **Foundations and frames (~13 to ~28 s).** The drums enter; the grid lights; foundations, columns and cages rise in
   waves on the beat; sparks fly; the camera begins to climb in a long crane move; grain recedes; sky starts to glow.
3. **Rise (~28 to ~40 s).** Floors stack, facades close, windows light up, the sun nears the horizon, fog lifts, the camera
   reaches a high aerial view and starts turning toward the final framing.
4. **Sunrise and words (~40 to 53 s).** The sun breaks over the horizon on a musical hit (a rapid but smooth brightening, no
   white-out, no flash) and the city is suddenly clear: long shadows, glass reflecting the sky. The camera locks into a wide,
   calm, symmetric frame of the skyline with the hero tower, and the three lines of the tagline appear in the sky above the
   skyline, one per musical event, as sunlit letters (screen-space composite from `tdist` with a soft glow and a subtle
   drop shadow is fine). Keep the bottom band dark and calm: the street level in shadow or a gentle gradient. After `fin`
   only slow cloud/haze drift and a slight camera breathing remain.

## Sound: cinematic, 90 BPM, D minor to D major, full score

Near-silent first 3 s; low drone and filtered-noise rumble, distant metallic clangs (short inharmonic bursts through a long
reverb). Taiko-like low drums (sine with pitch drop plus noise) enter with the frames phase; slow detuned-saw strings with a
chord progression (Dm, Bb, F, C or similar) that swells; low brass-like swells (filtered saws); a rising noise/tone riser
into the sunrise; the sunrise hit (low boom, cymbal-like noise, major lift); an FM-bell or glockenspiel motif, one note per
word (rising), and a final D major chord with the last word; long reverb tail, fade out. Use `K.duck` for a gentle pump.

Sync points to implement and list in your report: growth ring per bar, drums drive camera micro-shake (tiny) and spark
bursts, the sunrise brightening starts exactly on the hit, each word on its bell note, grain falls over the three phases.
