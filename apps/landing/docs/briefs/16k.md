# Production `16k` — "Traumhaus" (budget: 16 384 bytes minified)

Read `common.md` and `../CONTRACT.md` first. File: `src/p/16k.js`. Custom GL (`init`/`draw`), set `pts` (e.g. 262144).

## Picture: a cloud of light becomes a house, and the house becomes the sentence

About 250 k to 400 k GPU particles (`gl.POINTS`, `gl_VertexID`, no vertex attributes, additive blending), every position
computed in the vertex shader as a function of the particle index and `T`. Perspective is done by hand in the shader
(orbit rotation, `xy / (z + dist)`); soft round sprites in the fragment shader (`exp(-r*r*k)`), size by depth. Optional
and welcome if the budget allows: a cheap bloom (half-resolution blur of the particle pass through `D.rt`).

Palette: night violet and indigo, cold cyan sparks, warm gold and rose for the dream; the final text is gold-white. Colour
by phase, depth and a little per-particle hash.

Phases (guidance; put changes on bar lines):

1. **Dust (0 to ~13 s).** A dim turbulent cloud of cool particles drifting in a curl-noise-like flow, camera slowly moving
   through it, rare glints on the sound's clicks. Nearly black at t=0.
2. **Scaffold (~13 to ~28 s).** Particles are pulled out of the cloud onto a cubic lattice (nodes and edges) that assembles
   in front of the camera, then onto the *scaffold of a house*: wall edges, gable roof, door, windows, chimney, built from
   the ground up by a rising front, one storey or roof element per bar. The camera starts a slow orbit. The cloud around
   it thins as the house gains particles.
3. **Dream (~28 to ~40 s).** The house is complete and fills with warm light (walls become dense gold-rose particle
   surfaces), then begins to float and loosen: bricks levitate, the orbit widens, colours shift from cyan to gold. The music
   opens up (reverb, pads, melody). The house stays recognisable.
4. **Words (~40 to 53 s).** Particles leave the house in three waves and flow into the text: each particle owns a text target
   `tpt(i)`; the particles whose target lies on line k are released at the time of word k (so the house visibly dissolves
   into the sentence, and the last wave, `dreambau.com`, takes everything that is left). The camera eases to a frontal view,
   the text becomes a crisp dense gold-white particle typeface with a faint shimmer and a few rising embers. Keep the bottom
   band calm.

## Sound: dream-pop ambient, 100 BPM, C# minor to E major

Near-silent first 3 s, soft noise swell and distant glass-like pings in the first 13 s. A low heartbeat-like kick (low-passed,
gentle) enters with the scaffold; plucked arpeggio with ping-pong style delay and a long reverb; wide detuned pads that open
up in the dream phase; a simple singable melody (3 to 5 notes). One bell/pluck note per word (rising), a big soft swell
into `dreambau.com` and a warm major chord at its arrival; ring out, fade. Sidechain the pads gently against the kick.

Sync points to implement and list in your report: house storeys rise on bars; particle sparkle (size/brightness) on `E.x`
or the kick grid; word waves start on their notes; orbit speed/eases change on phase boundaries.
