# Production `4k` — "Rohbau" (budget: 4 096 bytes minified)

Read `common.md` and `../CONTRACT.md` first. File: `src/p/4k.js`. Tight budget: every byte counts, so write a lean shader
(use `frag:`) and a lean score. The runtime kit (`K.o`, `K.n`, `K.p`, `K.rev`, `K.duck`) is free; shader prelude helpers
(`h2`, `vn`, `fbm`, `tdist`, `rot`) are free.

## Picture: an isometric city that builds itself out of static

One full-screen fragment shader, 2D, no raymarching. An isometric (2:1 dimetric) grid of building columns, drawn the classic
way: for each pixel step down through the heights `k` (about 14 to 24 iterations), unproject the pixel to the ground cell it
would hit at height `k` and take the first cell whose column reaches that height. Top faces, left faces and right faces get
three shades. Heights come from a hash with a spatial envelope (taller towards the centre, streets every few cells) so the
result reads as a skyline. Windows are a hash pattern on the side faces; they light up over time.

Palette: ink-navy and deep teal shadows, cold cyan blueprint lines, amber/white lit windows and roof edges, a dawn gradient
(amber at the horizon, deep blue above) behind the city. One accent colour at a time.

Phases (times are guidance; put every change on a bar line of your tempo):

1. **Static (0 to ~13 s).** Television snow: hash grain with slow scan-line roll and a few bright specks; at t=0 nearly black,
   grain fades in. Faint ghost outlines of isometric cells flicker where buildings will stand. No order yet.
2. **Plan (~13 to ~28 s).** The beat enters. The cyan grid draws itself in as a radial wave, one ring per beat; the grain
   recedes in step. Columns start to rise in whole-floor steps on the beat (quantised heights, small overshoot), from the
   centre outwards. Noise is replaced by lines.
3. **City (~28 to ~40 s).** Heights reach their final values, the gradient sky comes up behind them, windows light up in a
   spreading wave, kick pulses make the window lights breathe (≤ 15 %), the camera slowly zooms out and drifts.
4. **Words (~40 to 53 s).** The music breaks down to chords. The city dims to a calm silhouette with a few lit windows, the
   three lines of the tagline arrive one per musical event in warm white with a soft amber glow; darken the city locally
   behind the glyphs so they stay legible. After `fin` only a slow window twinkle remains.

## Sound: compact minimal techno, 122 BPM, A minor

Clicks (Geiger-like noise ticks, density rising) and a filtered noise swell in the first 13 s, near-silent for the first 3 s.
Kick enters on the downbeat where the plan phase starts; off-beat hats, a sub bass line, plucked arpeggio with a dark delay
joins when the city appears, a metallic "hammer" accent on the last beat of each bar (construction as rhythm). Breakdown
when the words start: pad chord, one bell or pluck note per word (rising: A, C, E → final A major lift), riser into the
last word, then ring out and fade. Duck the pad against the kick.

Sync points to implement and list in your report: ring wave = one ring per beat; floor steps on beats; window pulse on the
kick; each word starts on its note; phase changes on bar lines.
